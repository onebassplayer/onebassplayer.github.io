Privacy Notice

Moonshot Exit Planner · Draft prepared September 23, 2026

Owner review required — not approved for publication This draft leaves retention, deletion handling, and email follow-up choices open rather than making claims we could not verify. Do not merge or publish it until the decisions below are confirmed.

Who operates this planner

This Exit Planner is operated by Moonshot Consulting. For privacy questions or to request deletion, email info@moonshotconsultingdc.com.

What you enter

Quick Plan: first name and email, employment situation and notes, accessible savings, monthly essential expenses, business-idea testing and notes, skills and experience, confidence, weekly time, and constraints.

Deep Dive: an email field and a free-text conversation. The current page sends the conversation transcript when you request a plan, but does not include the email field in that request.

Free-text answers can contain sensitive career or financial details. Share only what you are comfortable including.

How the information moves

When you request a Quick Plan, the page sends your first name, email, and assessment answers to Moonshot’s planning service hosted by Render. For Deep Dive, it sends the conversation transcript; the email field is not included in that request in the version reviewed.

The planning service is configured to forward accepted requests to Anthropic. In the version reviewed, the planner’s AI request is rejected by the service before it reaches Anthropic, so the page instead generates a fallback plan in your browser. This was checked on September 23, 2026 using test text, not personal information. If the model configuration changes, verify this explanation again.

The plan is displayed in your browser. The current planner code does not email the result.

Storage, retention, and deletion

The page code reviewed does not save these answers in browser storage, and the proxy code reviewed does not write them to an application database. This does not establish what the hosting services or any AI provider record in their logs or retain under their account settings.

No retention period or working deletion-request process has been verified. This notice must not promise a storage period, deletion outcome, or security control until those details are checked and approved.

Email and follow-up

The Quick Plan currently includes the name and email in its request to the planning service. The Deep Dive requires an email field, but the current code does not send that field with the conversation. The page says Moonshot may follow up, but its current code does not send follow-up email or call Mailchimp.

The proxy has a separate Mailchimp subscription route that marks an address “subscribed,” but the Exit Planner page does not call it. Whether the assessment should trigger personal follow-up, marketing email, or a Mailchimp subscription needs an explicit decision and a matching user choice before that flow is added.

Decisions required before publication
  • Confirm and document retention settings for Render request/service logs and Anthropic processing; do not infer these from application source code.
  • Confirm the privacy inbox is active and monitored, and verify who can fulfill deletion requests across any services that hold the data.
  • Decide whether email is for plan-related personal follow-up only or marketing too, and whether planner submissions should enter Mailchimp. If yes, define a clear opt-in and unsubscribe path first.
  • Resolve the planner/proxy model mismatch and verify the provider path before describing successful AI-generated results to users.
